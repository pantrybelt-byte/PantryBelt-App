# AccessBelt — Privacy Policy

*Effective Date & Last Updated: August 2026*  
*Contact Email:* getaccessbelt@gmail.com

---

## Our Promise to You
At AccessBelt, trust is essential. **We will never sell your personal data, and we never share it with third parties for marketing purposes.**

This policy applies to the AccessBelt website (`accessbelt.com`) and the AccessBelt mobile application. The mobile app operates without requiring account creation — you can locate food pantries completely anonymously.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### This Website (`accessbelt.com`)
If you sign up for waitlists or updates, we collect only your provided contact info (name, email address, phone number).

### The AccessBelt Mobile App
The app uses anonymous authentication by default. Optional data collection includes:
- **Coarse Location Data:** With your permission, coarse/rounded coordinates (approx. 1 mile resolution tied to a county) are used in-app to display nearby pantries and measure regional coverage.
- **User-Generated Content & Feedback:** Submissions regarding pantry updates, operating status, or comments.
- **Optional Profile Details:** Age group, family size, ZIP code, and optional race/ethnicity responses (all optional).
- **Pete AI Assistant Conversations:** In-app queries processed to recommend resources. PII is automatically stripped prior to logging.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** Device push tokens for opted-in alerts.
- **Aggregate Analytics:** Anonymous event metrics (pantry views, navigation clicks) aggregated by county for reporting impact to grant partners.

---

## 2. How We Use Information
Information is used to render nearby resource maps, power Pete AI recommendations, moderate user feedback, report aggregate community impact numbers to partner food banks, and send opted-in notifications.

---

## 3. Location Permissions
Location access is requested strictly while in-app ("When In Use"). You can decline or disable location permissions at any time; the app will default to a statewide map view.

---

## 4. Service Providers & Data Sharing
We do not sell data. We share data only with necessary operational infrastructure providers:
- **Google Firebase:** Anonymous auth, database hosting, and profile storage.
- **Google Gemini API:** AI assistant prompt processing (prompts are not used for public model training).

Aggregate, county-level impact statistics (e.g. total monthly pantry views) are reported to non-profit food bank partners. These reports never contain individual user data.

---

## 5. Security & Retention
We enforce TLS/HTTPS encryption and strict database security rules. Access is restricted to authorized personnel.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
AccessBelt is intended for users age 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

---

## 7. Your Data Rights & Deletion
You may delete optional profile data directly within the app settings or request complete data deletion by emailing `getaccessbelt@gmail.com`.

---
